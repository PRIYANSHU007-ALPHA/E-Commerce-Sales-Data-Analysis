
# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

### Data Analytics Project | Python • PostgreSQL • Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis** project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences, subscription trends, discounts, and revenue distribution.

The project uses a dataset containing **3,900 purchase records and 18 columns**. It follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and preparation in Python, performing business analysis using PostgreSQL, and presenting actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The main goal is to understand customer behavior and help businesses make data-driven decisions related to customer retention, marketing strategies, product promotions, and revenue growth.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing and spending patterns.
- Understand customer behavior across different age groups and genders.
- Compare revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Analyze subscribers vs. non-subscribers.
- Identify frequently purchased and highly rated products.
- Examine the impact of discounts on customer purchases.
- Compare average purchase amounts by shipping type.
- Segment customers based on their purchase history.
- Analyze repeat purchasing behavior.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize business insights.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project was completed in three major stages:

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation (Python)

Python and Pandas were used to prepare the raw dataset.

Key operations:

- Imported the dataset using Pandas.
- Explored the dataset using `df.info()` and `df.describe()`.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Imputed missing Review Rating values using the median rating of the corresponding product category.
- Standardized column names into snake_case format.
- Created an `age_group` column for customer segmentation.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature.
- Checked redundancy between discount and promo code columns.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.
- Connected Python with PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned dataset.

### 2. SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL)

PostgreSQL was used to answer 10 important business questions.

The analysis covered:

1. Revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. Customers using discounts while spending above average.
3. Top 5 products based on average review ratings.
4. Average purchase amount by shipping type.
5. Comparison of subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. Products frequently purchased with discounts.
7. Customer segmentation into New, Returning and Loyal.
8. Top 3 products within each category.
9. Relationship between repeat buyers and subscription status.
10. Revenue generated by different age groups.

SQL concepts used include:

- SELECT, WHERE and ORDER BY
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- Aggregate Functions (SUM, AVG, COUNT)
- CASE Statements
- Subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions and Ranking

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Revenue by Gender
- Male customers contributed 67.74% of total revenue.
- Female customers contributed 32.26% of total revenue.

### 2. Product Performance
- Blouse generated approximately 10.4K in revenue.
- Shirt generated approximately 10.3K.
- Dress generated approximately 10.3K.
- Pants generated approximately 10.1K.

### 3. Revenue by Category

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Clothing | 104,264 |
| Accessories | 74,200 |
| Footwear | 36,093 |
| Outerwear | 18,524 |

Clothing generated the highest revenue among all product categories.

### 4. Customer Age Group Analysis

- Young Adults generated the highest revenue (approximately 62K).
- Middle-aged customers generated approximately 59K.
- Adults generated approximately 56K.
- Senior customers generated approximately 56K.

### 5. Customer Subscription Analysis
- Non-subscribers contributed higher total revenue and customer counts.
- Average spending was relatively close between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### 6. Customer Loyalty
Customers were segmented into three categories:

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- Loyal Customers

Loyal customers represented the largest reported segment.

### 7. Discount Analysis
- Hats had the highest discounted-purchase percentage among the five products displayed.
- 839 customers were identified as discount users with above-average purchase amounts.

### 8. Shipping Analysis
Express shipping customers had a higher average purchase amount than Standard shipping customers in the reported results.
